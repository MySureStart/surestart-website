Benefits for Teachers & Schools

Professional Development in AI

Training and resources prepare teachers to understand AI basics, integrate tools into their classrooms, and guide students with confidence.

Curriculum Alignment

Programs are mapped to flexibly align with STEM, computer science, digital literacy, or interdisciplinary initiatives.

Enrichment Value

SureStart adds depth to existing offerings — whether as an elective, after-school program, or summer course — without requiring schools to redesign their curriculum.

Flexible Delivery Models

Options for semester, year-long, or summer sessions, delivered in hybrid or in-person formats, structured in 60-minute modular blocks fit seamlessly into your school calendar.

MIT FutureMakers: Student Leaders

Virtual • Annual Program

Implemented in collaboration with MIT RAISE, this annual 6-week virtual summer program engages middle school through college students from across the U.S. in AI learning. Participants develop AI fundamentals, build AI-driven solutions to real-world challenges, and gain career-ready skills and networks with industry experts.

AI at Branksome Hall: Opening Doors for Toronto Youth

Toronto, Canada. Summer 2025

During the summer of 2025, SureStart partnered with Branksome Hall in Toronto, Canada, to deliver multiple two-week AI intensives for high school students as part of the Slingshot AI Initiative. Students learned AI and Python programming in parallel, applying both to build innovative technical projects centered on local impact.
