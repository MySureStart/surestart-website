The Details:

Who it's for:

High school students who want to learn how to Vibe code effectively, build apps, solve real problems, and explore the intersection of AI, entrepreneurship, and social good. No prior coding experience required!

How it works:

Six 2-hour live Saturday sessions and six 1-hour independent challenges to be completed with your teammates or independently between sessions, with ongoing async support and collaboration via Discord.

Mentorship:

1:10 mentor-to-student ratio

Showcase:

Pitch your prototype at The Social Enterprise Symposium

Session Module Session Description
Week 1 Module 1: Intro to Vibe-Coding for Social Impact Learn to turn ideas into applications using AI-powered Vibe Coding tools. Explore UN Sustainable Development Goals, and build your first Vibe Coded app to address a SDG-aligned challenge.
Week 2 Module 2: Community-Centered Design & Social Entrepreneurship Apply human-first design and entrepreneurial thinking to solve real-world problems. Learn to apply user feedback and ethical frameworks to shape strong product–market fit.
Week 3 Module 3: Collaborative Development and Resource Management Prototype Vibe Coding apps to address real-world challenges, while exploring principles of successful collaboration and lean resource management through an understanding of token economics.
Week 4 Module 4: Backend Integration & Sustainable Solutions Discover how data, authentication, and backend systems power Vibe-coded apps to ensure security, scalability, and sustainable functionality.
Week 5 Module 5: Hands-on Project Development for Social Impact Bring your project to life through collaborative development with your team, and iterative refinement using Vibe tools. Learn to pitch and market your social impact solution.
Week 6 Module 6: Showcase & Reflection Present your completed Vibe Coding–based social impact project and reflections on your learning journey to peers, parents and invited guests.

By the end of the course, you'll have:

A fully functional app you built using Vibe Coding
Experienced the startup mindset through collaborative innovation
Gained awareness of the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
Developed a proof-of-concept for a social enterprise aligned with a UN SDG
Built confidence to launch, pitch, and grow your impact
A killer addition to your resume or portfolio
