Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 March 2026

SureStart ("we", "us", "our") operates the website mysurestart.com. This policy describes what personal information we collect, why, how we protect it, and your rights.

1. Who We Are

SureStart is an education organisation that provides AI and technology programs for students, universities, and K-12 schools. Our website is a static HTML site hosted on GitHub Pages at mysurestart.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Contact and enrollment forms — When you fill out an embedded Airtable form (contact, enrollment, Vibe Lab registration), you provide information such as your name, email address, school, and programme interest. This data is sent directly to Airtable's servers and processed under Airtable's privacy policy.
  • Vibe Lab registration data — Temporarily stored in your browser's localStorage (vibeLabRegistration) until submitted. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to us unless you complete the submission.

2.2 Information collected automatically (with your consent)

We do not collect any analytics or tracking data without your explicit consent. If you opt in to the Analytics category in our cookie banner:

  • Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID: G-CM0T1ZNC15) collects anonymised usage data including pages visited, time on page, referral source, device type, and approximate location (city level). We do not enable Google Signals, User-ID, or advertising features.

If you opt in to the Marketing category:

  • YouTube embeds may set cookies to track viewing behaviour and personalise ads. We use youtube-nocookie.com to reduce this tracking.

For full details of every cookie and similar technology, see our Cookie Policy.

2.3 Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not use advertising pixels (Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, etc.)
  • We do not use remarketing or retargeting
  • We do not sell or share personal information with data brokers
  • We do not use fingerprinting or hidden identifiers
  • We do not use AI-powered features (chatbots, personalisation, automated decisions) on this website

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use four categories of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Strictly Necessary — consent record storage (always on)
  • Preferences — Airtable forms, Vibe Lab localStorage (default off)
  • Analytics — Google Analytics 4 (default off)
  • Marketing — YouTube embeds (default off)

Full details including cookie names, lifetimes, and providers are in our Cookie Policy.

6. How to Manage Consent & Withdraw

You control your cookie preferences through our consent banner, which appears on your first visit with three equally prominent options: Accept All, Reject Non-Essential, and Manage Preferences.

To change or withdraw consent at any time, click the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of any page. This re-opens the same preferences panel. Withdrawing consent is exactly as easy as granting it.

When you withdraw consent for a category, we stop loading the associated scripts and embeds on subsequent page loads. Some cookies already set by third parties (e.g., Google Analytics) may persist in your browser until they expire — you can clear them through your browser settings.

7. Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We honour the Global Privacy Control signal. If your browser sends GPC, we automatically default all non-essential categories to off. You may still choose to opt in if you wish.

Static-site limitation: We detect GPC via the JavaScript API (navigator.globalPrivacyControl). The HTTP header (Sec-GPC) cannot be read because GitHub Pages has no server-side processing. All major GPC-capable browsers expose the JS API.

8. Data Retention

  • Consent record — stored in your browser's localStorage until you clear it or reset your preferences. Not sent to any server.
  • Google Analytics data — retained per Google's default retention settings (14 months for user-level data). We do not extend this period.
  • Form submissions (Airtable) — retained by SureStart in Airtable per our operational needs. Contact us to request deletion.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, via Cookie Settings in the footer

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

10. Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not participate in data broker marketplaces. We do not use advertising or retargeting pixels.

11. Children's Privacy

SureStart programs serve students including minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website. Form submissions from minors are handled through school and university partnerships with appropriate consent from parents or guardians.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through a notice on the website. The "Effective date" at the top of this page reflects the latest update.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us.
